Before accessing the course, you’ll need to create your account. Simply fill out the form with your first name, last name, and phone number. This ensures your profile is set up and ready to track your progress once you join

If you don’t have an account yet, you’ll be redirected to sign in first. Once inside, you’ll select your preferred payment method : Binance or BaridiMob. Here, you can also read more details about the course before confirming

After choosing your payment method, click “Confirm Request.” The website will provide you with the necessary payment details (Binance ID or BaridiMob RIP). Once you’ve sent the payment, simply send Ismail a quick WhatsApp message with proof — like a screenshot or transaction ID
